Safety Notes
Remove all props before testing. Motors run in the sequence A, B, C, D, starting clockwise from the front right. Hold the aircraft steady during any motor test. The "Stop all motors" button is always active — use it immediately if anything looks wrong.
⚠️
Safety Confirmation Required
Before running any motor test, confirm that:

① All propellers have been removed.
② The aircraft is secured on a flat surface.
③ All personnel are clear of the motors.

Motor tests will spin motors at the configured throttle. Proceeding without removing props can cause serious injury.
